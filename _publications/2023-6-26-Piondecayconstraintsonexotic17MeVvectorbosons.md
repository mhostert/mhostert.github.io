abstract: "We derive constraints on the couplings of light vector particles to all first-generation Standard Model fermions using leptonic decays of the charged pion, $\\pi^+\\to e^+ \\nu_e X_\\mu$. In models where the net charge to which $X_\\mu$ couples to is not conserved, no lepton helicity flip is required for the decay to happen, enhancing the decay rate by factors of $\\mathcal{O}(m_\\pi^4/m_e^2m_X^2)$. A past search at the SINDRUM-I spectrometer severely constrains this possibility. In the context of the hypothesized $17$~MeV particle proposed to explain anomalous $^8$Be, $^4$He, and $^{12}$C nuclear transitions claimed by the ATOMKI experiment, this limit rules out vector-boson explanations and poses strong limits on axial-vector ones."
